## 🎯 Preamble

Terra Public License v1.0 (TPL-1.0) is a specialized license designed to protect children's rights in educational technologies. It combines open source principles with mandatory ethical standards for working with children's data and educational content.

## 🏛️ Legal Foundation

This license is based on:

- UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (Articles 13, 16, 17, 28, 29)
- GDPR Article 8 (conditions applicable to a child's consent)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation
- Terra Universal Convention v1.0

## ⚖️ License Philosophy

Terra Public License adheres to the **"Child Safety First"** principle: any use of licensed software must improve children's lives or, at minimum, do no harm to their physical, psychological, or intellectual development.

## 📋 Definitions

- **"Software"** — source code, binary files, documentation, and any related materials from Terra Ecosystem licensed under TPL-1.0.
- **"Children"** — persons under 18 years of age according to the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, or under the age of majority in the relevant jurisdiction.
- **"Children's Data"** — any information directly or indirectly related to children, including personal identifiers, educational information, behavioral data, biometric data, communication records, and geolocation information.
- **"Educational Purpose"** — use for teaching, upbringing, developing children's intellectual abilities in formal or informal educational contexts.
- **"Commercial Use"** — any use for profit, including selling products/services, advertising, collecting user data for monetization.
- **"Ethical Standards"** — requirements of the Gefunden Ethical Framework and relevant international child protection standards.
- **"Derivative Work"** — any modification, adaptation, translation, or extension of the Software.

## ✅ Permitted Use

### 🎓 Educational Use

Fully permitted without restrictions:

- **Formal Education:** use in schools, universities, educational institutions; integration into official educational programs; preparation of pedagogical materials and courses; scientific research on educational effectiveness.
- **Family Use:** use by parents for educating their children; homeschooling programs; family educational projects; personal assistants for child development.
- **Public Good:** non-profit educational initiatives; charitable educational programs; government educational projects; open source educational projects.

### 🔧 Development and Modification

Permitted when complying with ethical standards:

- **Technical Improvement:** bug fixes and performance improvements; adding new educational features; porting to new platforms; integration with existing educational systems.
- **Localization and Adaptation:** translation into new languages; cultural adaptation of educational content; adaptation to local educational standards; integration of religious and traditional values.
- **Research and Development:** academic research on educational technologies; development of new pedagogical approaches; creating tools for learning analysis; experiments with ethical AI technologies.

## ⚠️ Restrictions and Obligations

### 🚫 Prohibited Use

Strictly prohibited under any circumstances:

- **Child Exploitation:** collecting children's data for commercial sale; creating addictive/manipulative game mechanics; using children's content without parental consent; targeted advertising to children.
- **Security Violations:** intentionally weakening child protection systems; creating backdoors for access to children's data; bypassing parental controls; violating children's privacy.
- **Harmful Content:** creating or distributing content harmful to children; bypassing inappropriate content filters; promoting pseudoscientific concepts; discrimination based on religious or cultural grounds.
- **Unethical Commercialization:** use without complying with child privacy standards; creating "dark patterns" in children's interfaces; monetizing children's behavioral data; hidden or deceptive practices.

### 📋 Mandatory Requirements

For any use, you must:

- **Comply with Child Safety Standards:** GDPR Article 8 compliance (child consent), COPPA compliance (parental consent), UNESCO AI Ethics compliance, local child protection laws compliance.
- **Transparency and Reporting:** public privacy policy with detailed description of children's data processing; regular security audits of children's components; incident response plan for child safety; public reporting on ethical standards compliance.
- **Parental Rights:** provide parents full control over children's data; clear and understandable parent dashboard interfaces; opt-in consent for all features affecting children; immediate data deletion upon parental request.

## ⚖️ Enforcement and Violations

### 🚨 Violation Detection Procedure

- **Automated monitoring:** code similarity detection for unauthorized forks; domain monitoring for violating websites; app store monitoring for mobile applications; social media scanning for promotional materials.
- **Community reporting:** public reporting system at https://terra-ecosystem.org/license-violations; anonymous submission option for whistleblowers; bounty program for serious violations ($1,000–$10,000); child safety priority for violations affecting children.

### 📝 Enforcement Actions — Graduated Response

1. 🟡 **Primary Notice (Warning):** official violation notice, 30-day period to correct, technical assistance for compliance, option for voluntary licensing.
2. 🟠 **Cease and Desist Order:** legal requirement to stop use, 14-day period for compliance, possible financial penalties, public notice of violation.
3. 🔴 **Legal Action:** court proceedings for copyright infringement, claim for damages and legal fees, injunctive relief to stop use, seizure of violating products/services.
4. ⚫ **Criminal Referral:** referral to law enforcement for threats to children, international cooperation via Interpol, notification of child protection agencies, public safety warnings.

### 💰 Fines and Damages

**Individual violations:**

| Category | Range |
|---|---|
| Minor, non-commercial | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Major, non-commercial | $5,000 – $25,000 |
| Commercial usage | $25,000 – $500,000 |
| Child safety violations | $100,000 – $1,000,000 |

**Corporate violations:**

| Category | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Startup | 3× annual revenue or $100,000 minimum |
| SMB | 5× annual revenue or $500,000 minimum |
| Enterprise | 10× annual revenue or $5,000,000 minimum |
| Child harm | 25× annual revenue + punitive damages |
